(c) Can the console restart explain the 8 misses? No.
Code: tm_usb_console_put() (lib/libtm/sysdepend/pico_rp2040/usb/
usb_console.c lines 268 to 285) appends to a 4 KB ring and, when the ring
is full, drops and counts the bytes; it never waits on the host. The USB
service task (priority 3, core 0) runs every 10 ms and, when the host is
not reading, stops writing without waiting (lines 175 to 202). An unread
console therefore loses output (boot 2's missing lines) but cannot stall
a task. Timing: the robot's silences on the wire are not tied to the
console. They go on after the reader restarted, for example 19:12:24.357
to 19:12:30.953 (6.60 s), while the console printed about 11 lines a
second (also about 11 a second during 19:08:16 to 19:08:24).

Observation, not a gate: robot uplink silences
The capture has 31 robot silences of more than 2 s (2.0 to 7.3 s), before,
between and inside the runs. In 26 of them the burst that ends the
silence starts with a retransmission of a segment the Mac had already
received and acknowledged at once, followed by new segments. This fits a
TCP acknowledgement from the Mac that did not reach the robot in time,
holding the robot's later segments (Nagle is on: neither lwIP's mqtt.c
nor mqtt_link.c turns it off) until lwIP's retransmission timer fired
(500 ms ticks, doubling). Both MQTT session losses in this session follow
such a silence. The cause of the lost or late downlink acknowledgements
is not established; the radio's power save, which stage B turns off, is
one candidate.
