S1 barrier change (e003301): disassembly of the status seqlock, single-core build
Date: 2026-10-26. Commit: e003301 (S1: drop the compiler extension from mqtt_link.c).

What this shows: with CNF_SMP=0 (SMP=0 NET=lwip MQTT=1) mqtt_queue_barrier() is an
empty function in mqtt_queue.c, and g_published and g_version are volatile. In both
functions below the status copy is a call to memcpy, and it sits between two calls to
mqtt_queue_barrier() and between the two accesses to g_version. No dmb instruction is
left in mqtt_link.o.

Source: git archive e003301, app_program/subsystem1/, with mqtt_config.h made from
mqtt_config.example.h (MQTT_LINK_CFG_SET 1, placeholder broker); these two functions do
not use the configuration.
